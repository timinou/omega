* Goal

Replace jj with beam-lisp native history, measured by the `omega.vcs` contract suite (§5.10). The redesign of autovcs starts from the native backend, not from jj.

* What the spike showed (OBSERVED, spike 3)

A five-verb `History` protocol over datom + `:db.type/file`: no-op checkpoints, queries by actor and by path, diff, and compensating abandon refused as `{:conflict paths}`.

* Gaps before it can replace jj

1. merges (two heads) and their conflict representation;
2. renames;
3. binary and large files over the blob tier;
4. git export/import at the human-decision tier (git stays the exchange format);
5. `annotate` as an index, not a parent walk;
6. large-tree performance (the spike walks parents linearly; needs a per-path latest-blob index);
7. a real change id scheme (the spike counts entities).

* Lessons to carry (§5.10, 1–6)

- a change is a fact with its intent written with it, so nothing needs mirroring;
- content addressing is datom's blob store;
- undo is compensation;
- split is a query over per-path attribution;
- the working copy is the buffers;
- git stays the exchange format.

* Acceptance

The contract suite is green on the native backend, including merges, renames and conflicts. `:vcs {:backend :native}` runs a real omega day without jj installed (note: jj is not even installed on this workstation today).
