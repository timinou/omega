* Symptoms (OBSERVED on omega's pin 2360b32e AND beam-lisp main 766fcb93)

1. **One entity updated N times costs O(N) to read and write.**
   - Both the memory and fjall stores, `/tmp/h/hot.bl.md` (reproduction inlined in spikes/history.bl.md Findings §4):
     - 100 updates: `entity` 9 ms, `transact!` 46 ms
     - 400: 51 / 130 ms
     - 1600: 161 / 514 ms
   - `lookup-ref` stays O(1), so the cost is in assembling the entity and the tx's current-value check over its history.
2. **Datalog joins and `entity` are far slower than the raw index.**
   - spikes/turn-read-cost.bl.md, 602 turns of one task:
     - a 4-clause query: 1398 ms
     - eids by query: 206 ms
     - the same eids via `(datom/datoms db :avet :turn/task t)`: 20 ms
     - `entity` per turn: ~0.6 ms each
     - three `:aevt` column scans: 30–40 ms each
3. **Related, beam-lisp runtime:** an `atom` deref copies the whole value out of its NIF cell (`BeamLisp.LazyMemo/read`). 100 `(get @a 5)` reads: 0.9 ms at 10 entries, 27 ms at 1000, 109 ms at 10000; the process dictionary is 0.3 ms flat (/tmp/h/atom2.bl.md).

* Impact

- omega long tasks slow themselves down: 1.2 s → 2.7 s per trivial step by step 160 (spikes/hot-task.bl.md).
- Any long-lived fact store design must avoid updating an entity in place; history refs had to become append-only.

* Where to file

Upstream in beam-lisp (datom + host refs). omega works around it (C8, append-only refs and usage) and does not depend on the fix.

* Acceptance (upstream)

- `entity` and single-attr `transact!` on an entity with 1600 prior updates are within 2× of a fresh entity.
- The 4-clause turn query over 602 turns is < 30 ms.
- `deref` cost is independent of value size, or atoms are documented as small-value-only.
