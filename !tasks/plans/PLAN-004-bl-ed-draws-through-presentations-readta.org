* Goal

bl-ed's screen is drawn by a pipeline of swappable pure BL stages instead of a fixed line renderer:
text ─read→ objects ─present→ marks ─layout→ slots+frames ─paint→ host. Packages (omega) add
readtable entries, presenters and modes; documents select among them; org stays the single source
of truth. The task view becomes block-like (turns as units, fisheye detail, a sticky composer), and
the say block is where you write.
