* 7B visual audit rubric
- Discoverability: primary action and current mode visible without instructions.
- Hierarchy: performance, preview and committed revision clearly distinguished.
- State: preparing/ready/playing/failed/cancelled distinguishable without color alone.
- Placement: controls beside their effects; destructive/irreversible actions distinct.
- Typography: readable scale, restrained labels, numbers aligned and meaningful.
- Layout: responsive Pocket use, usable touch targets, no occluded actions.
- Feedback: immediate response, visible progress, clear undo/cancel and recovery.
- Accessibility: contrast, keyboard focus, semantic names and reduced motion.
- Copy: explain only what visual structure cannot convey; keep essential labels.
