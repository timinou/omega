* Motion
Forest moves the way a plant does: slowly, then settled. Nothing bounces
except the moment something is handed over, the one celebration the product
allows itself. Every token below is playing at its real duration and curve,
then resting.

#+begin_motion :design forest
#+end_motion

#+begin_principles
Settle, don't slide | Things arrive by rising a few pixels and fading in. Nothing travels across the screen.
Extend in place | A card opens where it is (=unfold=). The sheet is the exception, for forms only.
One celebration | =hand-over= is the only overshoot: the check draws, the card greens and lifts. Done is worth a moment.
Work breathes | While the harness writes, the card breathes slowly (=draft=). Skeleton shimmers are retired.
Gates ripple | When a gate opens, a ring spreads from the strip and the newly opened card settles in after it.
Reduced motion is a mode, not a fallback | With reduced motion on, everything is instant except fades at or under 160 ms, which keep orientation.
#+end_principles

(defn- keyframes
  "Each motion token plays at its real duration and curve, then rests, so the
   eye can compare them; :loop tokens play without a rest."
  [m]
  (let [rest-ms (if (:loop m) 0 1400) total (+ (:ms m) rest-ms)
        p (fn [x] (str (/ (erlang/round (* 1000.0 (/ x total))) 10.0) "%"))
        e (str "animation-timing-function:" (:ease m))]
    (str "@keyframes fm-" (:name m) "{"
         "0%{" (:from m) ";" e "}"
         (when (:mid m) (str (p (/ (:ms m) 2)) "{" (:mid m) ";" e "}"))
         (p (:ms m)) "{" (:to m) "}"
         "100%{" (:to m) "}}"
         ".f-demo.fm-" (:name m) "{animation:fm-" (:name m) " " total "ms infinite}")))

** Parity with the app
Today the app's CSS is the original and this module copies it. This cell reads
the team machine's shipped stylesheets, when that checkout is present, and
reports any token here that has drifted (the kit's tests fail on drift). When the app generates its tokens from
this module (the work spine's roster item R9), the direction of the check
flips.

#+begin_src beam-lisp :id parity
(def app-root (Path/expand "~/code/products/ora-hub/team-machine/assets"))

(def sources
  "token → [stylesheet, custom property] in the shipped app."
  {:paper ["ora-base.css" "--paper"] :soft ["ora-base.css" "--soft"] :deep ["ora-base.css" "--deep"]
   :lime ["ora-base.css" "--lime"] :ink ["ora-components.css" "--ora-ink"]
   :secondary ["ora-components.css" "--ora-secondary"] :muted ["ora-components.css" "--ora-muted"]
   :forest ["ora-components.css" "--ora-forest"] :line ["ora-components.css" "--ora-line"]
   :inset ["ora-components.css" "--ora-inset"] :focus ["ora-components.css" "--ora-focus"]})

(defn shipped [[file prop]]
  (let [path (str app-root "/" file)]
    (when (File/exists? path)
      (let [m (Regex/run (Regex/compile! (str prop "\\s*:\\s*(#[0-9a-fA-F]{3,8})")) (File/read! path))]
        (when m (lower-case (nth m 1)))))))

(def checked (into {} (for [[k src] sources :let [v (shipped src)] :when v] [k [(get light k) v]])))
(def drift (into {} (filter (fn [[_ [ours theirs]]] (not= ours theirs)) checked)))

;; Reported, not asserted: the kit loads this module, and a drifted app must
;; not stop every dossier from rendering. kit_test.bl asserts it instead.
{:checked (count checked) :of (count sources) :drift drift}
#+end_src

* What is new here
#+begin_honest :title "Proposed, not shipped"
Dark mode and the motion vocabulary don't exist in the app today. The app has
no =prefers-color-scheme= rule, and its motion is two 150 ms transitions, a
skeleton shimmer, and dialogs and toasts that appear without animation. Both are
proposed here for you to react to. Everything under Palette (light), Type and
Shape is copied from what ships, and the =parity= cell keeps it honest.
#+end_honest
