* Search strategy
- Match Ora's demonstrated services to notices about AI, CRM/ERP, process automation, digital transformation, market strategy/communications.
- Morocco: official Portail Marocain des Marchés Publics (PMMP) advanced search, current consultations only, French keywords.
- France: PLACE (marches-publics.gouv.fr) is the state procurement platform; BOAMP content redirects to PLACE. Use official site, not aggregators.
- EU-wide: TED (ted.europa.eu) is the authoritative EU supplement; use buyer/official notice URL as the final evidence.
- Every final recommendation must have original buyer/portal evidence URL. Search-engine results are leads only.

* Source limitations
- PMMP is ASP.NET/Prado; refs and hidden form state change, so functions target stable element ids where possible.
- PLACE and TED may be client-heavy or block automation; report any block and try an alternate official source.
- Keyword search can miss notices that use equivalent terms (e.g. système d'information, transformation digitale, plateforme, automatisation).
