#+TITLE: DWIM · navigate what you see

* Read the shape, not the hidden source
This section is a navigation unit when folded.
The text remains intact; only its presentation changes.

** Nested detail
A child fold survives opening its parent.
Hidden source lines must never consume j/k steps.

* One shared visibility model
Rendering and movement consult the same outline spans.
No second parser. No DOM navigation. No server round trip.

* A removable interaction policy
h collapses an open heading; h again goes to its parent.
l opens a folded heading; l again enters visible content.
j and k move through visible source rows.
In prose, h and l still move character by character.

* The leverage
Navigation, selection, reveal, inspection and actions
can share one answer to: what is the thing here?

* Unload restores the key policy
:(ed/unuse "dwim") removes its keys, commands and goal state.
The document, your deliberate folds and your edits remain.
