* Assessment
DCE obtained by the preceding browser task and reviewed locally
(dce/RC.txt 26 PDF pages, dce/CPS.txt 24 pages). RC PDF page 5 (Article
4) states participant conditions generally (fiscal regularity, CNSS
affiliation, activity in relation to the object) and does NOT single out
domestic operators; the fiscal (percepteur du lieu d'imposition)
attestation, the CNSS regularity attestation and the RCCM modele 9 are
required under Article 5.I.A.2 only from the concurrent to which award
is envisaged (pieces listed on RC PDF page 6). RC PDF page 17 (Articles
15-16) sets the dirham as currency and grants a 15% domestic-preference
on financial offers for Moroccan-installed competitors; foreign or
non-installed competitors remain admissible. No mandatory Moroccan
registration/CNSS/fiscal requirement blocks a foreign bidder at offer
stage; the earlier wording overstated this. The two Qlik Sense/AI
attestations (RC PDF p.7) and four minimum team profiles (RC PDF p.8)
are UNKNOWN for Ora, and the 20,000 MAD provisional guarantee (CPS PDF
p.6) is unknown, so all requirement verdicts remain UNKNOWN and the
decision is needs-information.
