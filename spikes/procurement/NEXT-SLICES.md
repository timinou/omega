# Procurement roadmap after the first real-model prototype

Approved 2026-10-08. This document records scope, acceptance and deferred work;
completion evidence belongs in the slice report, not in this plan.

## Current slice: everyday decisions and bounded research

- Compact deadline-sorted shortlist with blockers, verification time and next action.
- Requirement review: retained source page/excerpt, company evidence, verdict,
  explicit correction/confirmation and a preserved review history.
- Missing-information work items: owner, due date, evidence attachment and review.
  Attaching a file does not automatically establish eligibility.
- Persistent research checkpoints, explicit partial/failure states and bounded
  resumption from saved evidence. Avoid blind retries and repeated acquisition.
- A browser/session shelf with owner, control, mode, profile, capture and actions.
- Clear fresh-workspace versus reuse actions; superseded narratives labelled.
- Keep all features in the same unloadable real-editor BL package, with separate
  domain/host files. No private application page or domain logic in vendor/bl-ed.

Acceptance: open a desk, identify attention items in thirty seconds, inspect a
blocker's exact retained source page, attach evidence and record its review;
run a fresh bounded discovery via real Omega/Flash without operator repairs.
Report the actual outcome if this last criterion fails; never equate tests with
unattended success. Preserve the local world boundary and commit as work lands.

## Deferred to subsequent slices

1. **Remote execution / Kernel adapter.** Implement provider lifecycle, remote
   artifacts and reconnection; prove equivalent local/remote behavior before
   calling remote execution supported.
2. **Full headed handoff.** Exercise real display-backed Chromium, takeover,
   switching and restoration. Current host has no desktop. Never claim that
   cookies/localStorage restore JS memory, drafts, sessionStorage or IndexedDB.
3. **Authenticated procurement portals and registration.** Account identity,
   expiring sessions and secret storage need a real portal test. No accounts are
   necessary for the present public-source slice.
4. **Scheduled monitoring and notifications.** First establish reliable bounded
   manual refresh; later add scheduling, deduplication and delivery preferences.
5. **Multi-user collaboration.** Local owner names are work-item metadata. Shared
   identities, assignment notifications, permissions and concurrent edits remain
   deferred and must not be implied by the current owner field.
6. **Broader discovery coverage.** The current slice proves a bounded country /
   source sample, not exhaustive Morocco/Europe coverage. Add evaluated portal
   adapters, selector repair and coverage reporting in later slices.
7. **Bid preparation and submission.** Buyer messages, applications, payments,
   signatures and submissions remain outside this research/decision scope.
8. **Production operations.** Multi-instance durable scheduling, disaster recovery,
   enterprise policy and production deployment are separate work.
9. **Independent legal/qualification assurance.** Citations and human review make
   interpretations inspectable; they do not certify eligibility or legal accuracy.

Deferred items should graduate only with their own implementation and evidence;
this list is retained even when a prototype offers related metadata or controls.
