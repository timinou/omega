#+TITLE: Atlas / design — an observatory for decisions
#+DATE: 2026-10-09

* Product intent
A market terminal makes facts available. A strategic desk should make the
reasoning between a fact and a decision visible. Atlas starts with a question,
shows competing explanations, and lets a person descend into evidence before
returning to an explicit choice. The notebook is the durable artifact; the
screen is a projection of it.

The specific question is whether Artcraft attention supports an Adobe short.
The general class is an emerging capability shock with uncertain business
transmission. The desk schema separates evidence, claims, a transmission
chain, scenarios, decision gates and research experiments. This can also
represent a pricing change, an open-source competitor, a regulation shock or
a supply-chain disruption.

* Precedents examined
** Observable: executable thought beside explanation
Observable's public notebook material demonstrates reactive exploration and
literate composition of prose, code and visuals. Atlas takes the adjacency
of source and result: an editable Org notebook remains beside its rendered
desk. A malformed specification produces a visible error instead of silently
falling back to attractive demo content.

[[https://old.observablehq.com/][Observable notebook overview]]

** Koyfin: persistent comparison and reusable watchlists
Koyfin's public dashboard and watchlist documentation emphasizes reusable
views, benchmarks and comparison. Atlas takes persistent context: a current
case stays visible while the analyst changes evidence or assumptions. It
uses a restrained information hierarchy rather than a full-screen wall of
undifferentiated numbers.

[[https://www.koyfin.com/help/mydashboards-myd/amp/][Koyfin dashboards]]

** Quartr: return to the source in context
Quartr's public transcript-search and highlighting descriptions emphasize
source context and returning to exact passages. Atlas takes the requirement
that a claim should expose its evidence dependencies, original URL and
locator. A click on an evidence ID leads to the corresponding evidence card;
the card distinguishes observation from source limitations.

[[https://quartr.com/features/transcript-search][Quartr transcript search]]

** Existing Omega spikes: one package, shared language
Workspace demonstrates panes and task context. Procurement demonstrates
accountable research, retained evidence and reusable functions. Browser
supplies a host boundary for visible research IO. Presentation demonstrates
rich documents without replacing the editor. Atlas composes the editor's
existing package-host and frame seams rather than creating a parallel app.

* Information architecture
** Observatory
Start with the decision state and the transmission bottleneck. Show the
strongest bear mechanism and strongest rebuttal side by side. Use a five-node
chain: attention, capability, adoption, economics and pricing. Unknown links
are visually present. They never receive fake confidence scores.

** Signal anatomy
A ledger explains what each observation can establish and what experiment
would discriminate between explanations. A chronology separates creation,
publication and capture times. HN discussions are expandable and linked.
The publication-cohort chart's caption prevents a common error: treating
retrieval-time scores as historical accumulation.

** Event comparison
A qualitative matrix appears with quantitative charts. The juxtaposition
makes differences inspectable: artifact, friction, economic mechanism and
market clock. Price charts expose dates and values on hover. Captions state
normalization, adjustment convention and the descriptive benchmark method.

** Evidence room
Claim cards lead to source cards. Filtering primary, secondary and gap
records supports an adversarial reading: what remains if headlines are
removed? An acquisition audit exposes endpoint, timestamp and hash. Source
links open in a new tab without navigating away from the notebook.

** Decision lab
Scenario payoffs and Bayesian sensitivity live beside decision gates and
experiments. Editable controls are assumptions, never sourced facts. Expected
P&L is contextualized by the worst listed scenario and the unbounded loss
outside the list. Gate failure is deterministic; favorable assumed P&L cannot
override missing capability or borrow evidence.

** Research journal
A contextual Omega task can ask a follow-up question. A memo draft records
what changed the analyst's mind and what would reverse the decision. Export
produces a .bl.org file containing rationale and current assumptions. This
makes deliberation portable without pretending transient state is saved.

* Visual language
A deep teal masthead anchors the case. Warm white cards sit on a muted paper
background. Teal marks observed structure; rust marks unresolved links.
Serif titles give narrative space while sans-serif tables carry observations.
The screen uses whitespace to separate arguments rather than relying on many
status colors. The palette is intentionally legible in screenshots and in a
long reading session.

At smaller widths, cards stack and the transmission chain becomes vertical.
The editable pane remains a real editor with its standard modes, save keys,
selection and command line. Buttons, inputs, select controls and expandable
source records use native browser semantics. No chart library is needed for
the current line charts; small SVGs keep offline operation and dependency
weight low.

* Interaction contract
- space s a opens the case notebook.
- space s q opens an owned research question prompt.
- Tabs change package state through BL events.
- Evidence-ID buttons select and scroll to a source record.
- Scenario controls update transient package state.
- Memo export retains the user's draft and assumptions as Org.
- Editing the atlas block changes the observatory from the same source text.
- Unloading atlas removes its state, owned layout and host resources.

* Deliberate constraints and next versions
Version one includes a fixed case study and a reusable schema. Some overview
labels and comparison series are still case-specific. Generalized arbitrary
watchlists require configurable role bindings, query specifications and a
case selector rather than merely changing the title. The schema is versioned
so that extension can fail explicitly.

The notebook does not auto-refresh. Refresh means collecting a new snapshot,
reviewing the diff and materializing a new proposal. This prevents a live
feed from overwriting a human's editorial work. A future feed can show fresh
observations in a separate layer before promotion.

The first iteration established source + projection + analytical functions.
The second adds real evidence, comparison, sensitivity, task follow-ups and
memo export. The proof tour checks both visual states and interactions in the
real editor. Further work should prioritize generalized case bindings,
additional public data adapters, evidence-diff views and experiment results.
