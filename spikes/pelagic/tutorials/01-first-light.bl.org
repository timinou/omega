#+TITLE: PELAGIC showcase 1 · first light

* Hear it
Press ▶ PERFORM, then C-Enter. This whole document is one runnable patch.
Listen for three soft notes repeating every four beats. If the built-in music
masks them, isolate the melody stem in the mixer.

#+begin_src beam-lisp :id first-light
(o/definst lantern [freq 440 amp 0.055 dur 0.7]
  (* amp
     (o/env-gen (o/perc 0.01 dur))
     (o/sin-osc freq)))

(def clock (o/metronome 120))

(av/session
  (av/configure {:bpm 120 :space 0.35 :echo 0.2})
  (av/pattern :melody 4
    (o/play
      (o/at (clock 0) (lantern :freq (o/midi->hz (o/note :D5))))
      (o/at (clock 1.5) (lantern :freq (o/midi->hz (o/note :A4))))
      (o/at (clock 3) (lantern :freq (o/midi->hz (o/note :F5)))))))
#+end_src

* Make three tiny changes
Change =:F5= to =:E5= and evaluate. Change =dur 0.7= to =dur 1.8= for overlap.
Change the middle beat from =1.5= to =1.75= for a different pocket.
UNDO restores the previous performance; your source text stays available to edit.

* What you just wrote
=definst= makes a reusable graph with named controls. Inside its body, =*=
constructs a graph operator. The oscillator and envelope become sound when the
host triggers a voice. Outside that body, arithmetic is ordinary BL arithmetic.

=clock= turns beats into relative milliseconds. =o/at= groups voices at a time;
=o/play= collects those groups; =av/pattern= gives the score a repeating stem.
The loop is four beats, so event positions must stay below four.
