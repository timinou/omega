#+TITLE: PELAGIC showcase 2 · pattern laboratory

* A little program becomes a score
Replace the source buffer with this document. Press ▶ PERFORM if needed, then
C-Enter. The melody climbs a Dorian scale while the bass marks an eight-beat loop.

#+begin_src beam-lisp :id pattern-laboratory
(o/definst prism [freq 440 amp 0.04 dur 0.5]
  (* amp (o/env-gen (o/perc 0.008 dur))
     (o/mix [(o/sin-osc freq)
             (* 0.2 (o/sin-osc (* freq 2.01)))])))

(o/definst floor [freq 110 amp 0.055 dur 0.8]
  (* amp (o/env-gen (o/perc 0.015 dur)) (o/sin-osc freq)))

(def clock (o/metronome 120))
(def pitches (o/scale :D4 :dorian))
(defn phrase [notes]
  (apply o/play
    (map-indexed
      (fn [index pitch]
        (o/at (clock index) (prism :freq (o/midi->hz pitch))))
      notes)))

(av/session
  (av/configure {:bpm 120 :space 0.45 :echo 0.25 :swing 0.1})
  (av/pattern :melody 8 (phrase pitches))
  (av/pattern :bass 8
    (o/play
      (o/at (clock 0) (floor :freq (o/midi->hz (o/note :D2))))
      (o/at (clock 4) (floor :freq (o/midi->hz (o/note :A2)))))))
#+end_src

* Explore quickly
Replace =:dorian= with =:minor= or =:major=. Replace =pitches= in the phrase call
with =(reverse pitches)=. Use =(o/chord :D4 :minor9)= there for a sparse ascent.
Each change is another complete patch evaluation.

The scale and chord helpers return MIDI numbers. =map-indexed= assigns a beat
to each number, and =apply= assembles the bundles into one play plan. These are
ordinary collection operations; you do not need a separate sequencer language.

* Reuse pays off
The instrument graph is prepared at definition time. Its shape is shared across
voices while =freq= and the other controls vary. Preparation caches and compiled
graph schemas live below this API, so playing more notes does not require you to
write a cache or manage an AudioNode pool.
