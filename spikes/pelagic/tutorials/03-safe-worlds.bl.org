#+TITLE: PELAGIC showcase 3 · safe worlds

* One patch, sound and image
Replace the source buffer with this document. Press ▶ PERFORM, then C-Enter.
The melody repeats while the built-in cathedral scene transitions in.

#+begin_src beam-lisp :id safe-worlds
(o/definst halo [freq 440 amp 0.045 dur 1.5]
  (* amp (o/env-gen (o/perc 0.015 dur))
     (o/mix [(o/sin-osc freq)
             (* 0.18 (o/sin-osc (* freq 3)))])))
(def clock (o/metronome 120))

(av/session
  (av/configure {:bpm 120 :space 0.55 :echo 0.3})
  (av/pattern :melody 8
    (o/play
      (o/at (clock 0) (halo :freq (o/midi->hz (o/note :D5))))
      (o/at (clock 2.5) (halo :freq (o/midi->hz (o/note :F5))))
      (o/at (clock 5) (halo :freq (o/midi->hz (o/note :A5))))))
  (av/variation {:seed 37 :density 0.8 :transpose 0})
  (av/visual {:intensity 1.2 :symmetry 6 :feedback 0.08})
  (av/launch :cathedral {:transition :iris :beats 8})
  (av/layer :afterglow 0.12))
#+end_src

* Audition without replacing the active revision
Open SESSION / MAPPINGS / DIAGNOSTICS. Change =:transpose 0= to =:transpose 7=
and =:density 0.8= to =:density 0.5=. Click AUDITION CODE instead of C-Enter.
You hear a short, quieter preview mixed alongside the active performance.
The active revision and its history stay unchanged.

Click CANCEL AUDITION to discard it. Click AUDITION CODE again, then COMMIT
AUDITION to activate it on a future bar boundary. If you change the live
performance in between, that preview becomes stale: audition again before
committing. Preview cleanup also happens on package unload.

This is a monitored preview, not a copy of the recording mix. The preview bus is
excluded from the main recording. Keep the active arrangement sparse when you
want to compare pitches closely.

* Play the world
Try BUILD, BREAK, DROP and RELEASE, then UNDO. Save a named snapshot in the
expanded controls; change scenes and recall it. The scene transition controls
choose a musical boundary, transition shape and duration.

=seed= makes density decisions repeatable; =transpose= is in semitones.
=feedback= retains some of the previous image. Avoid raising many controls at
once: change one, listen, then keep or undo it.
